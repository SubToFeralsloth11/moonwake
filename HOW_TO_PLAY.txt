1. QUICK START
---------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Start the game. You begin with 300 Shells and a 12-card starter
    deck called "Starter Tides".
 2. Walk (WASD) to the Notice Board and press E.
 3. Challenge the first opponent, Pip Barnacle.
 4. Win your duel to earn Shells.
 5. Walk to the Gilded Gull shop, press E, and buy a Harbor Pack
    (100 Shells).
 6. Walk to the Cartographer's Desk (Deck Desk), press E, and swap
    the new cards into your deck.
 7. Keep beating opponents to unlock new regions.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
2. CONTROLS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
 In the harbor:
   W A S D / arrow keys   move
   Shift                  sprint
   Space                  jump
   E                      interact (use the shop, desk, board, or a
                          person)
   Esc                    menu

 In a duel:
   Click a card in your hand, then click a shrine on your side to
   play it there.
   Click a card you just played (still face-down) to take it back.
   "End Turn"             lock in your plays and reveal
   "Ring the Moon Bell"   double the stakes
   "Retreat"              give up now


---------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE HARBOR (HUB)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
 Gilded Gull (shop)         buy card packs and card backs
 Cartographer's Desk        build decks, craft cards, break cards
                            into Glimmer
 Notice Board               campaign opponents, Tavern Ladder,
                            daily quests
 Old Tactician              another way to open the campaign

4. DUEL RULES
---------------------------------------------------------------------
 THE GOAL
   There are 3 shrines on the table. A duel lasts 6 rounds. After
   round 6, whoever has more power at a shrine wins that shrine.
   Win 2 or more shrines to win the duel.
   If you each win the same number of shrines, the higher total power
   across all 3 shrines wins. If that is also tied, the duel is a draw.

 CARDS
   Every card has:
     - a Glow cost (what it costs to play)
     - a SUN value (power under the Sun)
     - a MOON value (power under the Moon)
     - sometimes an ability
   Each shrine shows a sky, either Sun or Moon. A card only uses the
   value that matches its shrine's sky.

 DECKS AND HANDS
   A deck is exactly 12 cards. You start each duel with 4 cards in
   hand. Your hand holds at most 8; if it is full, you skip the draw.

 THE SKIES
   At the start, each shrine is randomly Sun or Moon, but never all 3
   the same.

 EACH ROUND
   1. DRAW: each player draws 1 card.
   2. GLOW: you get Glow equal to the round number (1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6),
      plus any bonus Glow from abilities. Unspent Glow does NOT carry
      over to the next round.
   3. PLAY: both players play cards face-down at the same time. A
      shrine holds 4 cards per side unless its text says otherwise.
      You can take back a card until you press End Turn.
   4. REVEAL: the player winning more shrines reveals first (random if
      tied). Cards flip one at a time and their "Reveal" abilities
      trigger in that order.
   5. DUSK (after rounds 1-5 only):
        - "Dusk" abilities trigger (grow, fade, etc.)
        - shrine dusk effects happen (Bonfire, Whirlpool)
        - EVERY shrine's sky turns over: Sun becomes Moon and Moon
          becomes Sun. Still Waters, Lighthouse Keeper, and "anchor"
          cards stop this.
        - Drifting cards move to another shrine.
   After round 6 reveals, there is no dusk. The duel is scored.

 THE MOON BELL (STAKES)
   - The stake starts at x1.
   - Ring the bell to double it (x2, x4, up to x8). The new stake
     applies from the next round.
   - Only one player can ring it each round.
   - A bigger stake means more Shells for the winner and more Renown
     won or lost on the Ladder.
   - If you ring the bell, the AI may retreat.

 RETREAT
   Give up now and lose at the CURRENT stake instead of the doubled
   one. Use it when a doubled duel is clearly lost.

5. CARD ABILITIES
---------------------------------------------------------------------
 TIMING WORDS
   Reveal:     happens once, when the card flips face-up
   Ongoing:    always active while the card is on the table
   Dusk:       happens at the end of each round (rounds 1-5)
   When moved: happens when the card is pushed or drifts

 REVEAL ABILITIES
   Burn X         Enemy's strongest card here gets -X.
   Burn all X     All enemy cards here get -X.
   Burn wide X    Enemy's strongest card at EVERY shrine gets -X.
   Boost X        Your other cards here get +X.
   Rally X        Your other cards from the same court, at every
                  shrine, get +X.
   Sky-up         Gain +X if this shrine is under the named sky.
   Draw X         Draw X cards.
   Glow X         +X Glow next round.
   Summon         Add token cards to this shrine (if there is room).
   Slay           Destroy the enemy's weakest card here.
   Push           Move the enemy's strongest card here to another
                  shrine.
   Scatter        Move EVERY enemy card here to other shrines.
   Swap           Swap the Sun and Moon values of every card here
                  (both sides).
   Leech X        Take X power from the enemy's strongest card here
                  and give it to this card.
   Hand X         The cards in your hand get +X.
   Still          This shrine's sky never turns again.
   Devour         Destroy your own weakest other card here and add
                  its values to this card.
   Conjure        Add a specific card to your hand.
   Mirror         Copy the values of the enemy's strongest card here.
   Flip           Turn this shrine's sky.
   Flip other     Turn the skies of the other two shrines.
   Dawn           Every shrine turns to Sun.
   Night          Every shrine turns to Moon.

 ONGOING ABILITIES
   Aura X         Your other cards here get +X.
   Court aura X   Your other cards from the same court, everywhere,
                  get +X.
   Sky X          +X while this shrine is under the named sky.
   Lone X         +X if it is your only card here.
   Crowd X        +X for each other card you have here.
   High           Always uses its higher value.
   Anchor         This shrine's sky can't turn.

 DUSK ABILITIES
   Grow X         Gain +X.
   Grow sky X     Gain +X on the value of the current sky.
   Drift          Move to another shrine.
   Tidal X        Gain +X whenever this shrine's sky turns.
   Rouse X        Your other cards here get +X.
   Fade X         Lose X.
   Burn X         A random enemy card here gets -X.

 Power can't go below 0 for devour, but burns can push a card's
 values into negative numbers.

6. THE 15 SHRINES (3 are picked at random each duel)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
 Quiet Shrine      No effect.
 Sunwell           Cards here get +2 under the Sun.
 Moonpool          Cards here get +2 under the Moon.
 Still Waters      This shrine's sky never turns.
 Whirlpool         At dusk, each side's weakest card here is destroyed.
 Ember Altar       Ember cards here get +2.
 Coral Gate        Tide cards here get +2.
 Elder Root        Grove cards here get +2.
 Windspire         Gale cards here get +2.
 Narrow Pass       Only 2 cards per side.
 Lonely Peak       Only 1 card per side.
 Lantern Market    Cards played here cost 1 less.
 Restless Tide     Its sky turns every time a card is revealed here.
 Bonfire           At dusk, every card here gets +1.
 Mirror Lake       Cards here always use their higher value.
 (A duel never has both Narrow Pass and Lonely Peak.)


---------------------------------------------------------------------
7. THE 5 COURTS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
 Ember Court  (orange)  Blazing under the Sun. Burns enemy cards down.
 Tide Court   (blue)    Strong under the Moon. Turns the skies.
 Grove Court  (green)   Starts small, grows every dusk, crowds the
                        shrine.
 Gale Court   (lilac)   Restless. Moves between shrines and pushes
                        foes away.
 Wildlings    (gold)    Strange wanderers that bend the rules.

 Rarities: Common (grey), Rare (blue), Epic (purple), Mythic (orange).
 There are 100 collectible cards.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
8. MONEY
---------------------------------------------------------------------
 SHELLS: the main currency. Spend them on packs and card backs.
   You start with 300.
   Ways to earn them:
     - Win a campaign duel: opponent's reward x stake.
       First win against an opponent: extra bonus + their reward card.
       Rematch wins pay half.
     - Win a Ladder duel: opponent's reward x stake.
     - Lose: 10 Shells.   Draw: 20 Shells.
     - Daily quests: 40-90 Shells each.
     - Collector milestones and Ladder rank rewards.

 GLIMMER: crafting dust. Use it to craft the exact card you want.
   Ways to earn it:
     - Pull a card you already own at the maximum copies: it turns
       into Glimmer automatically.
     - Break (disenchant) cards you don't want.
     - Collector milestones.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
9. BUYING PACKS, STEP BY STEP
---------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. In the harbor, walk to the Gilded Gull shop.
 2. When "Browse the Gilded Gull" appears, press E.
 3. Pick a pack:
      Harbor Pack    100 Shells  5 cards from any court, at least
                                 1 Rare or better
      Ember Pack     140 Shells  5 Ember cards, at least 1 Rare+
      Tide Pack      140 Shells  5 Tide cards, at least 1 Rare+
      Grove Pack     140 Shells  5 Grove cards, at least 1 Rare+
      Gale Pack      140 Shells  5 Gale cards, at least 1 Rare+
      Moonlit Pack   450 Shells  5 cards, at least 2 Epic or better,
                                 higher Mythic chance
 4. Click to buy. The pack opens and shows each card. "NEW" marks a
    card you didn't own; extra copies over the limit show as Glimmer.
 5. Close the shop. Your cards are now in your collection; go put them
    in a deck (section 11).
 Tip: when you know which court you want, buy that court's pack.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
10. CRAFTING AND BREAKING CARDS, STEP BY STEP
---------------------------------------------------------------------
 Craft costs (Glimmer):   Common 20 | Rare 60 | Epic 200 | Mythic 800
 Break value (Glimmer):   Common 5  | Rare 15 | Epic 50  | Mythic 200
 Max copies you can own:  Common 2  | Rare 2  | Epic 2   | Mythic 1

 TO CRAFT:
 1. Walk to the Cartographer's Desk and press E.
 2. Find a card you don't own yet, or own fewer than the max copies of.
 3. Click "Craft" (it shows the Glimmer cost).
 TO BREAK (DISENCHANT):
 1. At the same desk, pick a card you own.
 2. Break it to get Glimmer. If a deck no longer has enough copies of
    that card, the card is removed from that deck.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
11. BUILDING A DECK, STEP BY STEP
---------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Walk to the Cartographer's Desk and press E.
 2. Click cards in your collection to add them; click cards in the
    deck to remove them.
 3. A deck must have EXACTLY 12 cards, and you can only use copies
    you own (max 2 of a card, 1 for Mythics).
 4. You can save several decks. Set one as active; the active deck is
    the one you take into duels.
 Good deck shape: about 4 cards that cost 1-2, 4 that cost 3-4, and
 4 that cost 5-6, using 1 or 2 courts.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
12. CAMPAIGN, STEP BY STEP
---------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Go to the Notice Board (or talk to the Old Tactician) and press E.
 2. There are 6 regions with 5 opponents each (30 total):
      The Harbor Steps -> Emberreach Forge -> Tidehollow Grotto ->
      The Grovewild -> Galespire Heights -> The Eclipse Court
 3. Beat opponents in order. Beating one unlocks the next.
 4. Beat all 5 in a region to unlock the next region.
 5. Your first win against each opponent gives bonus Shells, and most
    also give a reward card.
 6. Each region's fifth opponent is its boss. Opponents in later
    regions are smarter and have stronger decks.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
13. TAVERN LADDER (RANKED)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. On the Notice Board, click "Tavern Ladder duel vs a random rival".
 2. Win: +2 Renown x stake.   Lose: -1 Renown x stake.   Draw: no
    change.
 3. Every 6 Renown = 1 rank. Ranks go from 0 to 50.
    Titles: Deckhand, Bosun, Navigator, Captain, Admiral (each I-X),
    then Tidewright at rank 50.
 4. Every 5th rank gives bonus Shells. Every 10th rank gives Shells
    and a free pack (a Moonlit Pack from rank 30 up).
 5. Rivals get smarter as your rank goes up.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
14. QUESTS, MILESTONES, AND CARD BACKS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
 DAILY QUESTS: you get 3 new quests every day (see the Notice
 Board). Each pays Shells automatically when it's finished. Examples:
   Win 2 duels (60), Play 20 cards (50), Play 8 cards of one court
   (50), Win after the Moon Bell rang (70), Win all 3 shrines (90),
   Destroy 3 enemy cards (50), Open a pack (40), Play 3 Ladder duels
   (60).

 COLLECTOR MILESTONES: every 10 different cards you own earns bonus
 Shells and Glimmer, and the reward grows with each milestone.

 CARD BACKS (cosmetic only, bought at the Gilded Gull):
   Harbor Classic free | Forge Flame 250 | Deep Current 250 |
   Mossy Bark 250 | Cloudsail 250 | Starry Night 600 |
   Royal Eclipse 1200


---------------------------------------------------------------------
15. STRATEGY TIPS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
 - Spend all your Glow every round; leftover Glow is wasted.
 - You only need 2 shrines. Give up the hardest one and put your
   cards on the other two.
 - THE BIG ONE: skies turn at every dusk, but not after round 6. In
   round 6, play for the sky you can see. In earlier rounds, the sky
   will change before the final count.
 - Sun cards (Ember) like Sunwell; Moon cards (Tide) like Moonpool.
   Mirror Lake makes the sky not matter.
 - Revealing second is an advantage: your Burns and Pushes hit cards
   that are already revealed.
 - Ring the bell when you're clearly ahead. Retreat when a doubled
   duel is lost.
 - Save your big 5-6 cost cards for rounds 5 and 6.
